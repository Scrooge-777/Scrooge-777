    This model re-implements the CorDapp's contract rules, flows, notary and vault logic in JavaScript, with real SHA-256 hashing and ECDSA P-256 signatures from your browser's Web Crypto. Each node holds its own private key, and every node that receives a transaction re-checks its id, its signatures, its back-chain and the contract before storing it.
